Headcount plans that survive a shutdown week

Annual headcount files go stale the first time a furnace trips. A living plan tracks three numbers weekly: people at work, people absent, and tickets that only a named craft can close.

We keep that view with plant HR so outsourcing top-ups are a decision, not a surprise invoice.
